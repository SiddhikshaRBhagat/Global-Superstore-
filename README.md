# 🌎 Global Superstore – Retail Sales & Business Performance Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Global Superstore – Retail Sales & Business Performance Analytics** project is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze retail sales, profitability, regional performance, product trends, customer behaviour, and shipping operations.

The project transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive dashboards, KPI cards, charts, and filters.

The main objective is to help business stakeholders understand sales performance, identify profitable products, evaluate regional performance, and make data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify top-performing regions and countries.
- Evaluate product category and sub-category profitability.
- Understand the impact of discounts on profitability.
- Analyze customer segments and purchasing behaviour.
- Evaluate shipping methods and delivery performance.
- Support better business decision-making through data visualization.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard development and data visualization.
- **Power Query** – Data transformation and preparation.
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Calculated measures and business KPIs.
- **Global Superstore Dataset** – Retail sales data.

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview

Provides a high-level summary of overall business performance.

**Key Performance Indicators:**
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity
- Average Order Value
- Profit Margin

**Visualizations:**
- Monthly Sales and Profit Trend
- Sales by Customer Segment
- Profit by Region
- Sales Performance Analysis

### 2. Sales & Regional Analysis

Focuses on geographical and regional sales performance.

**Key Performance Indicators:**
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Countries
- Regional Profit Margin
- Average Sales per Order
- Sales Growth %

**Visualizations:**
- Sales by Country
- Sales by Market
- Regional Sales Performance
- Global Sales Map
- Yearly Sales and Profit Margin
- Monthly Sales Trends

**Filters:**
- Country
- Region
- Segment
- Market

### 3. Product & Profitability Analysis

Analyzes product performance and identifies profitable and loss-making products.

**Key Performance Indicators:**
- Average Discount
- Loss-Making Products
- Sales Contribution %

**Visualizations:**
- Sales by Product Category
- Profit by Sub-Category
- Product Performance Analysis
- Discount vs Profit Scatter Plot
- Quantity and Sales by Sub-Category

**Filters:**
- Order Date
- Category
- Sub-Category
- Segment
- Market

### 4. Customer & Shipping Analysis

Evaluates customer performance and shipping operations.

**Key Performance Indicators:**
- Total Customers
- Total Orders
- Average Shipping Cost
- Average Shipping Days
- Total Sales

**Visualizations:**
- Orders by Shipping Mode
- Average Shipping Cost by Ship Mode
- Order Priority Distribution
- Sales and Customer Segment Analysis

**Filters:**
- Order Date
- Segment
- Ship Mode
- Order Priority
- Market

## 📈 Key Business Questions

This project helps answer the following business questions:

1. Which region generates the highest sales?
2. Which product category contributes the most revenue?
3. Which sub-categories generate the highest and lowest profits?
4. How are discounts related to profitability?
5. Which countries contribute significantly to overall sales?
6. Which customer segment generates the highest revenue?
7. Which shipping mode is most frequently used?
8. How does shipping duration vary across shipping methods?
9. Which products are generating losses?
10. How does business performance change over time?

## 💡 Business Value

The dashboards provide useful insights to support:

- Sales performance monitoring.
- Product and pricing optimization.
- Regional business performance evaluation.
- Customer segment analysis.
- Shipping performance monitoring.
- Identification of profitability opportunities.

## 📌 Conclusion

The Global Superstore project demonstrates how Power BI can be used to transform retail sales data into interactive business intelligence dashboards.

By combining sales analysis, regional performance, product profitability, customer behaviour, and shipping analytics, this project provides a comprehensive view of business operations.
